Solis data attribution

World map: Natural Earth public-domain country boundaries, distributed by
datasets/geo-countries (https://github.com/datasets/geo-countries).
Geometry is simplified and rendered using an equirectangular projection.

Fire data shown in the app comes from the Space Monkeys API: NASA FIRMS MODIS
C6.1 and VIIRS 375 m active fire products (historical record, Fires now and
forecast input) and NOAA GOES-19 ABI fire detections (real-time anchor of the
7-day forecast). Biomes: IBGE Biomas do Brasil 2019, 1:250,000.

The full list of datasets, references, libraries, licenses and the use of
Super Intelligence (SI) is on the About the data page.

MODIS and VIIRS sensor descriptions reference public NASA FIRMS documentation:
https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/

Solis is an independent NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 concept.
