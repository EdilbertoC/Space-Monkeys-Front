OrbitBurn demo data attribution

World map: Natural Earth public-domain country boundaries, distributed by
datasets/geo-countries (https://github.com/datasets/geo-countries).
Geometry is simplified and rendered using an equirectangular projection.

Hotspot coordinates, detection counts, trends, comparisons, and alerts are
synthetic illustrative data. They are not NASA observations.

MODIS and VIIRS sensor descriptions reference public NASA FIRMS documentation:
https://firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/

OrbitBurn is an independent NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 concept.
No NASA affiliation, endorsement, or live data connection is implied.
